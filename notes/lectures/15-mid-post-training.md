# Lecture 15: Mid/Post-Training

## Keywords

- Supervised fine-tuning (SFT)
- Mid-training
- Human preference data
- RLHF
- Direct preference optimization (DPO)

## Summary

The lecture explains how a pretrained text predictor becomes an instruction-following assistant. Supervised fine-tuning depends heavily on demonstration quality, with datasets evolving from converted NLP tasks toward conversations, expert responses, and structured tool use. It discusses hallucination risks, safety tuning, and the growing role of mid-training in introducing specialized capabilities. Preference learning collects rankings from humans or models, trains reward signals, and shapes behavior through reinforcement learning or direct preference optimization. PPO and DPO illustrate different algorithmic routes. Annotation quality, demographic influence, judge biases, and reward overoptimization remain central challenges. The overall pipeline succeeds through carefully chosen data and feedback, not algorithms alone.
