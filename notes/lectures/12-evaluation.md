# Lecture 12: Evaluation

## Keywords

- Perplexity
- Exam benchmarks
- Human and model judges
- Agent evaluation
- Benchmark validity

## Summary

The lecture treats evaluation as the process of turning desired capabilities into concrete measurements. Perplexity measures predictive quality, but does not capture every aspect of useful behavior. Exam benchmarks test knowledge and reasoning with comparatively clear grading, while chat benchmarks rely on human preferences or model judges and introduce stylistic and demographic biases. Agent benchmarks add environments, tools, and scaffolding, so results reflect more than the underlying model alone. Safety evaluation introduces further contextual judgments. Across these settings, contamination, flawed questions, incomplete tests, and benchmark saturation threaten validity. Inspecting responses and traces helps determine whether a metric actually measures the intended capability.
