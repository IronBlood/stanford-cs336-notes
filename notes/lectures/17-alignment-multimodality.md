# Lecture 17: Alignment - Multimodality

## Keywords

- CLIP and SigLIP
- Vision transformers
- Vision-language adapters
- Multimodal position embeddings
- Image tokenization

## Summary

Despite the filename's alignment label, this lecture focuses on multimodal models, especially connecting images to language models. CLIP learns aligned image and text representations through contrastive training, while SigLIP uses pairwise binary classification. Vision transformers turn image patches into embeddings. LLaVA and Qwen illustrate how adapters connect pretrained vision encoders to language models, with staged training, curated data, dynamic resolution, video handling, and multidimensional positional information. The lecture also examines discrete image tokenization as a route to joint text-image generation. A recurring distinction is that semantic understanding and detailed generation need different representations, while modality balancing and context length constrain practical designs.
